About UU Assignment Cover Generator

Standardized, print-ready cover pages built for Uttara University students.

Project Overview

The Uttara University Assignment & Lab Report Cover Page Generator was created to solve a persistent frustration experienced by university students: the tedious, repetitive formatting of cover pages for course assignments, laboratory reports, project proposals, and term papers across all departments (CSE, BBA, EEE, Textile, Civil, English, Law, Mathematics, etc.).

Instead of struggling with inconsistent Microsoft Word templates, misaligned table borders, distorted logos, or missing faculty designations, students can now generate perfectly formatted, university-compliant, ready-to-print A4 cover sheets in under 30 seconds.

Key Innovations & Features

Student Portal Auto-Fill

Log in with your UU ERP Student Portal credentials to auto-populate Student Name, Student ID, Department, Section, and active academic semester.

Smart Course & Faculty Linking

Selecting any registered Course Title auto-completes its Course Code, assigned faculty member, designation, and department instantly.

Real-Time A4 Live Preview

True-to-scale interactive preview with zoom controls, customizable margins, frame borders, and custom logo upload support.

PDF & Ultra-HD PNG Export

One-click direct browser printing to standard A4 PDF or lossless high-resolution PNG image download for digital submissions.

3-Semester Calendar Intelligence

Uttara University operates on a three-semester academic cycle per calendar year. The generator automatically detects the active academic semester without manual intervention:

  • Spring Semester: January 1 – April 30
  • Summer Semester: May 1 – August 31
  • Fall Semester: September 1 – December 31

Privacy & Security First

Unlike traditional web forms that store student submissions in third-party databases, the UU Assignment Cover Generator is engineered with a strict client-first privacy architecture:

  • All student assignments, grades, faculty names, and generated documents are rendered locally in your web browser.
  • Portal login credentials are exclusively used via SSL encryption to authenticate against the student ERP and are never permanently stored or logged on external servers.
  • Saved presets and preferences live entirely in your device's private LocalStorage.
